Frequently Asked Questions

The UH Sugar Land Student Center is a space on campus where students can study, meet with groups, grab food, and relax between classes.

The Center is open to UH Sugar Land students, faculty, and staff. Visitors are welcome in the common areas.

The Center has five bookable rooms, from small study rooms for one or two people to larger rooms for club and group meetings. You can see each room's capacity and description on the Room Booking page.

Go to the Room Booking page, choose a room, and fill out the form with your name, number of attendees, date, and start and end times. Then click Submit.

You'll see a message right away. If the room is available for your date and time, your reservation is confirmed. If it's already taken, you'll be told it was declined so you can pick a different room or time.
